# GeoTrivia Question Review

150 numbered pairs / 300 playable questions.

Each number covers one fact with an Easy and Hard version. Tell me which numbers to keep, remove, or rewrite. Specify Easy or Hard if your choice applies to only one version. Anything you do not mention stays unchanged. No questions have been removed.

Example: "Remove 12 and 38. Keep 20, but make its Easy clue simpler."

## Contents

- Sports: 1-15
- Geography: 16-91
- History: 92-115
- Art & Architecture: 116-131
- Pop Culture: 132-150
